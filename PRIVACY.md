# Privacy policy: Simple Aisle for Home Assistant

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This integration runs entirely inside your own Home Assistant. We, the developer, never receive any of your data.

**What it handles, and where it stays**
- The grocery items of the stores you choose to sync, on your Home Assistant's own lists.
- To keep those lists in step: a record of which list item matches which Simple Aisle item, changes waiting for a
  phone, and a short history of applied changes. These are stored in your Home Assistant's own storage.
- For each paired iPhone or iPad: a name you can see (such as "Alex's iPhone"), its app version and when it last
  synced. Its key is stored only as a one-way hash.

**What it doesn't do**
- It doesn't send anything to the developer or any other server. There are no analytics, ads or tracking.
- It doesn't have Simple Aisle accounts. Simple Aisle connects straight to your Home Assistant, using the addresses
  you configured there.
- It never touches lists you didn't link, or anything else in Home Assistant.

**Your control**
- You choose which stores sync, and private stores never do.
- Revoke any device from the Simple Aisle panel.
- Removing the integration deletes its own storage. Your lists and their items stay.
- How Home Assistant is reachable from outside your home (Home Assistant Cloud, a reverse proxy, a VPN) is up to your
  own Home Assistant setup and that service's terms.
